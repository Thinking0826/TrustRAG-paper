# Notice

This repository is a public research snapshot prepared for inspection and reproducibility. It contains original TrustRAG code and de-identified study records, plus selected material derived from third-party public datasets.

No separate open-source licence is granted for the authors' original code or study records in this snapshot. Public availability does not waive copyright or grant reuse rights beyond those provided by applicable law and the upstream terms below.

QASPER-derived source text and references originate from Dasigi et al., “A Dataset of Information-Seeking Questions and Answers Anchored in Research Papers,” NAACL 2021, and are governed by CC BY 4.0. This release removes annotation-worker metadata and replaces email addresses and social-media handles in redistributed source slices and recorded model outputs with neutral placeholders. The official unmodified dataset is available at <https://huggingface.co/datasets/allenai/qasper>.

MultiFieldQA-en originates from Bai et al., “LongBench: A Bilingual, Multitask Benchmark for Long Context Understanding,” ACL 2024. Its source documents, questions and references are not redistributed here; the restoration script expects the authors' official archive. See <https://huggingface.co/datasets/zai-org/LongBench>.

Model weights are not included and remain subject to their upstream licences.
